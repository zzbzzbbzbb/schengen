# JOPE Official Source Map

Primary and authoritative sources behind the JOPE-specific facts injected into
this skill pack. **Policy refresh date: 2026-10-03.** In this refresh the Springer
(`link.springer.com`), GLO (`glabor.org`), and UNU-MERIT (`pop.merit.unu.edu`) pages were
**not directly readable** (network egress blocked); facts were taken from search-engine
snippets of those pages, from the Editor-in-Chief's public annual reports, from Wikipedia,
and from SpringerLink article records surfaced by search. Each fact below carries a
verification status. Re-check the live pages before submission-ready advice because
editors, collections, fees, and policy wording change.

Status key: **✔ official** = official page snippet or EiC report; **◐ secondary** =
Wikipedia or third-party site; **✖ unverified** = not located in this refresh; treat as a
default to confirm, not a fact.

## Official journal & policy pages

- **https://link.springer.com/journal/148** — Springer journal home. Aims and scope, editors,
  submission-to-first-decision metric, open-access model, current special collections, updates.
- **https://link.springer.com/journal/148/submission-guidelines** — Submission guidelines. Abstract
  rule, title page, source-file requirements, declarations, research-data policy, references, peer
  review model, ethics and AI policy.
- **https://link.springer.com/journal/148/editors** — Editorial board.
- **https://link.springer.com/journal/148/how-to-publish-with-us** — Open-access options and charges.
- **https://link.springer.com/journal/148/updates** — Journal updates, including Kuznets Prize
  announcements and editor awards.
- **https://www.editorialmanager.com/jope/** — Submission system (Editorial Manager).
- **https://glabor.org/platform/journal-of-population-economics/** — GLO's JOPE page: editors,
  editorial office contact, journal description.
- **https://pop.merit.unu.edu/jpop/** — POP at UNU-MERIT's JOPE page; Kuznets Prize history at
  **https://pop.merit.unu.edu/jpop/kuznets-prize/** and **https://glabor.org/kuznets-prize/**.
- **Editor-in-Chief annual reports** (posted on glabor.org and klausfzimmermann.de): submissions,
  acceptance rates, decision times, impact factor.
- **https://ideas.repec.org/s/spr/jopoec.html** — RePEc series page (handle `spr:jopoec`), useful for
  verifying that a paper appeared in JOPE.
- **https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Population_Economics** — founding, ISSNs, indexing.

## Operational notes for this pack

- The **150-word non-technical abstract** is the one hard, verified, venue-specific limit; every
  skill that touches the abstract enforces it.
- The **review model, submission fee, APC amount, length cap, and code-deposit rule** are unverified
  in this refresh. Say so when advising; point the user to the live submission guidelines.
- Acceptance-rate and decision-time figures are from EiC reports through 2021 plus the Springer page's
  current median; present them as a range, not a promise.
- The **special-collection list changes**; re-read the journal home before recommending a collection.
- Re-check the editors page before naming an editor in a cover letter.
